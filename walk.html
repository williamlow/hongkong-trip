A WALK THAT WORKS AS A DAY

Leave room
for the harbour.

Think of this as a full-day wander. The moving time is much shorter than the day: museums, lunch, market browsing, queues and the ferry fill the gaps. Reaching the 8pm lights may mean a pause for dinner.

  1. MORNING

    Art, park, then a snack.

    Start at the Museum of Art, wander through Kowloon Park, then follow stops 3–4 toward Jordan. Check the museum’s opening day and exhibition arrangements before setting off.

  2. LUNCH & AFTERNOON

    Browse the old market streets.

    Take your time around the Jade Market, Tin Hau Temple, fruit-market exterior and Shanghai Street. The Jade Market is a daytime market; Temple Street’s evening stalls are a different experience.

  3. AROUND DUSK

    Cross the harbour, if you fancy.

    The photographed itinerary includes a ferry outing after stop 9. A Tsim Sha Tsui–Central return trip is a straightforward version; allow time for two crossings, waiting and boarding. Check the operator’s current timetable and weather notices.

  4. 8PM · HONG KONG TIME

    Avenue of Stars and the skyline.

    The published usual start of A Symphony of Lights is 8pm. The promenade around the Cultural Centre and Avenue of Stars offers harbour views; bad weather or announcements can cancel the show. Check the current notice rather than treating it as guaranteed.

Route accuracy, timing and what has changed

The photo does not show the book’s edition or publication date. We have preserved its ten-stop numbering while matching current branch/place descriptions where available. Street lines and distances are planning estimates; they do not certify a step-free route or current access through a park, building or crossing.

The theatre detour is exterior-only: the Tourism Board page currently carries a closure/construction notice. Shanghai Street is represented by its Yau Ma Tei kitchenware section, not by 618 Shanghai Street. The fruit-market pin is the wholesale complex near Waterloo Road, not a similarly named fruit shop farther south. No fixed shop, temple or museum opening hours are assumed.
