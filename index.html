A FEW USEFUL THINGS

Eat like you’ve
been here before.

01 / THE BILL

Keep some cash handy.

Small shops may prefer cash. Prices here are rough HK-dollar estimates per person or snack order, not checked menus; drinks and extra dishes add up.

02 / THE TABLE

Small tables, quick meals.

Sharing a table is common in busy cafes. Have your order ready, and expect efficient rather than leisurely service.

03 / THE ORDER

One bowl, then wander.

Share small orders when practical. Leave space for cheung fun, a warm egg tart, or a bowl of tong sui around the corner.

04 / BEFORE YOU GO

Check the exact branch.

Opening days, addresses and queues change. Use the Google Maps link to check recent activity. Ask the shop about ingredients if you have dietary restrictions.
