## Location and route checks

- Place coordinates chiefly come from Tourism Board location metadata. The waffle shop, Mak Man Kee, Shanghai Street and specific ferry piers use matched address/OpenStreetMap locations. Eaton comes from the existing hotel data.
- Rejected ambiguous geocoder matches: an ordinary fruit shop south of the wholesale complex and a similarly named jade market in Sham Shui Po. The fruit-market pin is by Waterloo Road/Reclamation Street, around 22.31229, 114.16861. The Yau Ma Tei jade-market pin is around 22.30925, 114.16995.
- The walking line is manually traced along approximate street/park corridors and joins all ten pins in book order. Its calculated length is **about 5.9 km**, excluding detours, the Eaton approach and ferry. It is illustrative, not surveyed geometry or live routing, and does not certify crossing, entrance or step-free access. Allow a full day for visits and the evening finish.
- Google Maps walking links split the itinerary into **1–4, 4–8 and 8–10**. Each uses at most three intermediate waypoints for mobile compatibility. All ten stops are represented. Optional detours have individual map links.
- An optional schematic ferry layer connects Tsim Sha Tsui with Central Pier 7. The suggested excursion returns to Tsim Sha Tsui before stop 10; the line is not the vessel's exact track.
- KML exports **18 point placemarks** (ten original stops, six additions, Eaton and Central Pier 7), the approximate walking line and the schematic ferry line. XML escapes place names and writes longitude before latitude. Compatible tools include Google Earth and Google My Maps; the file does not provide live navigation.
- Visited state uses the browser-local key `one-more-bite-kowloon-walk`. Progress counts only the original ten stops. Clearing it preserves food favourites. Storage failure falls back to session memory. No accounts or external progress storage.

Browser checks cover numbering/route continuity, exact branch queries, directions waypoint limits, layer controls, stop-to-popup navigation, persistence and blocked storage, KML XML/geometry, map tile failure and mobile layout. These establish software behaviour, not current admission or business operations.
