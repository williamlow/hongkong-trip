# Four neighbourhood day guides

Checked **1 November 2026, Hong Kong time**. The user supplied a pasted conversation looking for the atmosphere of an independent creative neighbourhood: galleries, interesting shops, coffee and characterful cocktails. The user clarified that **each neighbourhood should support a rounded day, including food and drinks**, rather than being limited to one activity. The four guides therefore mix those interests in every day plan.

## Structure and scope

`neighbourhoods.html` is a hub linking to separate pages for Sham Shui Po, Sheung Wan, Sai Ying Pun and Wong Chuk Hang. There are **34 numbered places** across the four guides (9, 11, 9 and 5 respectively). Categories describe individual stops; they do not limit a neighbourhood to one role. Africa Coffee & Tea appears under both coffee and drinks because the source describes a café/bar. Its current bar service needs checking.

Every guide includes a flexible lunch/afternoon/coffee/evening plan, local-food options, practical transit information from Eaton, terrain notes, opening/access caveats, individual Google Maps address links and a short walking section with no more than three intermediate waypoints. Routes are suggestions with alternatives, not bookings or claims of a current event.

## Map and implementation checks

- Tourism Board location metadata is used for relevant single-place listings. OpenStreetMap/Nominatim matched street or address results support the other pins; ambiguous results were inspected rather than blindly choosing the first result. Apliu Street uses the market segment around Kweilin Street, rather than its southeastern road segment. Blue Supreme uses Tung Street in Sheung Wan, not a similarly named address in Kowloon.
- The exact 110 Queen’s Road West result supports Mostly Harmless. CODA's First Street and Winstons' Queen’s Road West positions are explicitly approximate. Pins are for planning, not guaranteed entrances; Maps links always contain full branch addresses.
- Maps show numbered place locations, not a guessed walking line through buildings. The hub adds an Eaton hotel pin and four neighbourhood pins. Category changes keep cards, map markers and result counts in sync. The café/bar dual category is intentional.
- All resource URLs are relative for the `/hongkong-trip/` Pages path. Generated static HTML contains the full day plans, sources and external links even without JavaScript. Leaflet is vendored; OSM attribution remains visible.
- The shared header has four tabs on all pages and stays visible at tablet widths. Food-card fragment links scroll to the card after the food module has created it.
- Tests cover balanced categories, citation integrity, route waypoint limits, all four pages and navigation, filters/map sync, correct branch popups and keyboard focus, food-guide deep links, Eaton transit links, explicitly unverified leads, static content without JS, blocked tile fallback, phone/tablet overflow and marker refitting.

No gallery appointment, restaurant booking or third-party message has been made. Research establishes published context and addresses, not today's operations. Venue website links are operator links for the user's final check, not a claim that their live programmes were independently fetched.
