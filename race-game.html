A LITTLE TIMING GOES A LONG WAY

Find the middle.
Keep your horse moving.

Share one keyboard or use the two touch panels. Willy is player one; Gilly is player two. A three-second countdown gives you both time to get ready.

  1. Hit the centre

    The meter fills from 0% to 100% and back. Press your stride button at 50%: A for Willy, K for Gilly. Closer timing means more speed. A wide hit can slow you down.

  2. Let the next sweep begin

    Each hit locks the meter briefly, then starts a fresh sweep. The 0.9-second cooldown means rapid tapping will not beat good timing. Your boost lasts a few seconds, then fades towards normal pace.

  3. Watch for an injury

    Each horse can pick up two temporary injuries during the middle laps. Press S to recover Willy or L to recover Gilly as soon as the alert appears. Recovery clears the slowdown immediately; unattended injuries clear after eight seconds.

  4. Race all four laps

    The first horse across the line wins; an exact photo finish can be a draw. Press P or use Pause whenever you need a break. Switching tabs or leaving the window pauses the game until you resume.

Gentle meters give both players more time. Reduced-motion preferences select Gentle by default and remove decorative galloping; the timing game still runs. Meter speed stays fixed for the current race. Randomness changes injury locations, while your timing and recovery decide the race.
